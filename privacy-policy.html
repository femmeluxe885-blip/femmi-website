This Privacy Policy explains how Winmoon Technologies ("we", "us") handles information when you use Femmi. Femmi is intended only for adults aged 18 and above. Do not use the app if you are under 18.

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legitimate business needs, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or comply with legal obligations. Any information retained after an account deletion request will be limited to what is legally or legitimately necessary.
