Request deletion of your Femmi account

If you no longer wish to use Femmi, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to submit a request

  1. Use the email address associated with your Femmi account, where possible.
  2. Include your registered mobile number or account identifier.
  3. Write "Femmi Account Deletion Request" in the email subject.
  4. Send the request using the button below.

Request Account Deletion

What happens next?

We will verify your request and contact you if additional information is needed. Once verified, we will process deletion of the account and associated personal data, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Data retention

Some records may need to be retained for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, payment disputes or other legitimate obligations. Any such retention will be limited to the applicable purpose and period.

In-app deletion

You should also be able to initiate account deletion from the account or settings section inside the Femmi app. This website request option is available if you cannot access the app.
