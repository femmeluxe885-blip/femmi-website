STRICTLY 18+ ONLY

Winmoon Technologies

Effective Date: 7 November 2026

Femmi is exclusively for adults aged 18 years and above. Children and individuals under 18 are not permitted to use or register for the Femmi application.

1. Our Commitment to Children's Safety

Winmoon Technologies is committed to protecting children from inappropriate access to adult-oriented services. Femmi is designed and intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and above.

2. Age Restriction

Individuals under 18 years of age are strictly prohibited from creating an account, accessing or using Femmi. Users must provide accurate information during registration.

3. Prevention of Underage Access

Femmi is intended to apply age eligibility checks during account registration and use. Accounts reasonably suspected of belonging to underage individuals may be reviewed and appropriate action may be taken.

4. Reporting Suspected Underage Accounts

If you believe that someone under 18 is using Femmi, please report the account through available in-app reporting tools or contact our support team.

Please provide relevant details so that we can investigate. Do not send unnecessary personal information about a child.

5. Investigation and Action

We will review reports of suspected underage use. Where an account is reasonably determined to violate our age requirements, we may restrict access, suspend or remove the account and take appropriate action.

6. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Femmi does not permit content or conduct that exploits, endangers or sexually abuses children. Suspected child sexual abuse material or child exploitation must be reported immediately through our support contact.
